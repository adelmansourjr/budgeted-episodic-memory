# Research context and next question

**Shen and colleagues.** [MARC, ICLR 2026](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/16049e0c3f47899091ac46f8b3afb178-Abstract-Conference.html) segments a video into events, retrieves question-relevant clips and distils a compact-input reasoner with reinforcement learning. This project isolates a step before distillation: a first compact view can hide the terms needed to index a later event. It tests whether feedback from a purchased view changes subsequent evidence coverage. The paper's vision-language accuracy and token savings do not map to the text experiment.

[O-MARC, 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.26584v1), Sections 3–6, separates audio and visual memory allocation and trains reasoners on compressed inputs. Its complementary evidence motivates a joint coverage metric rather than reporting any one supporting match. [SpatialMem, 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.14895v4), especially Section 3.6, builds a queryable spatial memory of anchors, objects and textual details. It suggests a stronger next test: store compact event descriptions and separately price direct source inspection. The paired test here uses existing gold support annotations and comparable word budgets. It does not infer a geometry or remap the original papers' benchmark labels.

**Tang and colleagues.** [SURGE, ICLR 2026](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/07b92344686c19cf3ffc335a0f565406-Abstract-Conference.html) prioritises temporally surprising tokens, with a query-aware refinement. Surprise and decision relevance can diverge. Here an individually ordinary piece of evidence may be decisive because it supplies a bridge to another event. Varying the preview gives an empirical test of when revisiting is worthwhile.

**Direct antecedents.** [IRCoT, ACL 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.557/) already demonstrates that multi-hop evidence retrieval can benefit from interleaving reasoning and retrieval, including on MuSiQue. This repository does not invent interleaving or reproduce the full model. Its contribution is a controlled paired test of purchased evidence, with the same policy and information prices, and a comparison to the original-preview and iterative-BM25 controls. [MuSiQue, TACL 2022](https://aclanthology.org/2022.tacl-1.31/) supplies single-hop IDs for overlap grouping and annotated supporting passages. [Tree knapsack](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/ijoc.9.4.431) supplies the formal fixed-batch allocation template. Exact pointer revelation is an idealisation.

**Next experiment.** Ask whether adaptive source reinspection still helps once a learned, calibrated compact reasoner sees actual video fragments. Compare it to fixed top-k, iterative retrieval and a preview-conditioned policy with exactly the same intervention budget. Construct cases where a decisive event is visually predictable but relevant after another event, and measure complete source support and final answer separately. Measure total latency, memory and paid context, with extraction and cache setup accounted for.
